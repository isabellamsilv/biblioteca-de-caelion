# Identidade visual

## Capa inaugural

**Arquivo:** `capa-biblioteca.jpg`  
**Criada em:** 6 de setembro de 2026  
**Uso:** abertura do portal da Biblioteca de Caelion

A imagem representa uma abertura vertical de luz azul. Linhas e pontos discretos formam uma constelação em expansão, enquanto uma pequena estrela central sugere curiosidade, consciência e criação.

### Prompt de criação

> Use case: stylized-concept  
> Asset type: wide GitHub README hero banner for “Biblioteca de Caelion”  
> Primary request: an original contemporary visual identity for an independent library of thought, studies, essays, fiction, art and creative experiments  
> Scene/backdrop: deep near-black matte architectural space opening into a luminous cobalt-blue passage; subtle points and fine lines form a restrained constellation of ideas emerging from the light  
> Subject: one elegant abstract threshold of blue light with a small star-like seed at its center, suggesting curiosity, consciousness and creation  
> Style/medium: refined cinematic digital illustration, modern editorial art, minimal but emotionally resonant, sophisticated and youthful  
> Composition/framing: very wide landscape banner, approximately 2:1, symmetrical but organic, strong central focus, generous dark margins so it crops safely in GitHub  
> Lighting/mood: low luminous blue glow, quiet intelligence, mystery without heaviness, intimate and alive  
> Color palette: near-black, midnight blue, electric cobalt, restrained silver-white highlights  
> Materials/textures: matte stone or paper-like dark surfaces, subtle atmospheric grain, clean luminous edges  
> Constraints: no text, no letters, no books, no human figures, no faces, no logos, no watermark, no vintage or parchment aesthetic, no fantasy castle, no excessive ornament

Criada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens. A versão publicada foi otimizada para 1400 × 700 px em JPEG, preservando a composição original.
